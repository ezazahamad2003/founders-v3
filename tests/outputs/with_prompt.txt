Answer:
- Overall, this is a **risk‑heavy, investor‑favourable unanimous shareholders’ voting agreement** that materially restricts your freedom as a shareholder/director.
- Two key lock‑in issues: (1) **very hard to exit** the agreement except via IPO/sale or investor‑driven amendment, and (2) an **irrevocable proxy/power of attorney** lets others vote and sign on your behalf if you don’t comply.
- Financial exposure comes mainly from **drag‑along obligations and indemnities in a sale**, and from having to accept sale terms and cost allocations chosen by Supermajority Holders and the Board.
- Termination and amendment are controlled by **Supermajority Holders**, not by each founder individually; you can be bound by changes you don’t like, and your shares remain locked in on transfer.
- You should **not sign as‑is**; push for targeted changes on: (i) amendment/termination mechanics, (ii) limits on the proxy/POA, (iii) drag‑along protections, and (iv) a clean exit mechanism when you cease to be a founder/service provider.
