Q1 — General Contract Review

You are reviewing a commercial contract where your client is the Customer / Paying Party. Identify the top 5 legal and commercial risks in the agreement. For each risk, quote the clause, explain why it is problematic for the Customer, and propose a reasonable redline.

Q2 — Liability & Indemnity Focus

Review this agreement and identify any clauses that expose the Customer to disproportionate liability or weak indemnification protection. Explain the risk and propose improved language.

Q3 — Termination & Exit Risk

Analyze the contract for termination, renewal, and lock-in risks. Which provisions could trap the Customer in an unfavorable relationship, and how would you revise them?

Q4 — Payment & Financial Exposure

Identify clauses that could create unexpected payment obligations, penalties, or revenue leakage for the Customer. Explain the risk and suggest contract edits to limit financial exposure.

Q5 — Data, IP, and Confidentiality

Review the agreement for data protection, confidentiality, and intellectual property risks affecting the Customer. Highlight problematic clauses and propose corrections that align with standard market practice.
