Principal Amount: $__________

then this Note shall automatically convert into the same securities issued in that financing at a conversion price equal to the lower of:

The price per share in the Qualified Financing multiplied by (1 – ___% discount), OR

Accrued interest converts with principal.

3. Optional Conversion Upon Non-Qualified Financing
If the Company completes an equity financing that does not meet the Qualified Financing threshold, the holder may elect to convert at the discount and valuation cap described above.

4. Change of Control

Repayment of principal plus accrued interest, OR

If the Note has not converted prior to the Maturity Date, the holder may elect:

Repayment of principal plus accrued interest, OR

Conversion into common stock at the Valuation Cap price.

Plain-English Explanation (Important)
Most Favored Nation (MFN)
If you later give another investor better note terms (lower cap, higher discount, etc.), this investor can upgrade to those better terms.

Why include it?

Early investors expect fairness.

Prevents renegotiation drama later.

Market standard in NYC/SF.

Pro Rata Rights
If your investor converts into equity, they can invest more in the priced round to maintain their ownership percentage.

Example:
If they own 5% after conversion, they can buy enough shares in the Series A to stay at 5%.

Why include it?

Serious angels expect it.

It signals confidence.

It does not hurt you early stage.
