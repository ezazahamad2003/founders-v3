Why you might need more than a purchase agreement
Even after a purchase agreement for equity, most startups also use a Separation Agreement (sometimes called a “Release and Settlement Agreement” or “Termination and Release Agreement”) with these common components:

Mutual release of claims → prevents future litigation

Confidentiality/Non‑disclosure reaffirmation → covers proprietary info after departure

Intellectual Property Assignment → clarifies that all IP created by the founder belongs to the company

Non‑solicitation/Non‑competition provisions (if applicable & enforceable)

Return of Company Property

Transition obligations (knowledge transfer, timelines)

Survival clauses (what obligations survive termination)

Because Ontario contract law and employment standards can impact enforceability (especially restrictive covenants), it’s best to run this by counsel.

📄 Founder Separation and Release Agreement — Template (Ontario)
This Founder Separation and Release Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made as of [Date] between:

[Company Name], an Ontario corporation with its principal place of business at [address] (“Company”),
and

[Departing Founder Name] of [address] (“Founder”).

1. Background
A. The Founder has been a co‑founder and shareholder of the Company.
B. The Company and Founder have entered into a Purchase Agreement dated [date] pursuant to which the Founder sold certain shares to the Company.
C. The Parties now wish to finalize the Founder’s departure, including mutual releases and post‑departure obligations.

2. Definitions
In this Agreement, unless the context requires otherwise:

“Confidential Information” means all non‑public information of the Company;

“Intellectual Property” means all inventions, designs, know‑how, workflows, code, models, processes, ideas, and proprietary materials created by Founder relating to the Company’s business.

3. Separation
3.1 Effective Date of Separation: The Founder’s separation from all roles (director, officer, employee, consultant) is [Date].

3.2 The Founder confirms they have received all compensation due through the Effective Date.

4. Mutual Release
In consideration of the agreements herein, each Party releases the other from all claims, demands, obligations, and causes of action of any kind arising before the Effective Date, including those relating to employment, equity, fiduciary duties, or corporate roles.

The release does not apply to obligations that expressly survive this Agreement.

5. Confidentiality
5.1 The Founder acknowledges ongoing obligations under any existing confidentiality or IP agreements.

5.2 The Founder agrees not to disclose, use, or exploit the Company’s Confidential Information at any time.

6. Intellectual Property
6.1 The Founder confirms that all Intellectual Property created during involvement with the Company has been assigned to the Company.

6.2 To the extent any IP was not previously assigned, the Founder irrevocably assigns it now.

7. Non‑Solicitation and Non‑Competition (if applicable)
Note: enforceability of restrictive covenants in Ontario is case‑specific and should be reviewed by counsel.

7.1 For a period of [duration] after the Effective Date, the Founder will not:
(a) solicit Company employees or contractors;
(b) solicit Company customers or clients.

7.2 [Optional Non‑Competition clause — tailored by counsel]

8. Return of Property
The Founder agrees to return all Company property by [date], including devices, documents, keys, and credentials.

9. Transition Assistance
The Founder will reasonably assist in transferring knowledge and responsibilities to Company representatives through [date/period].

10. Survival
Clauses 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, and this Section survive termination.

11. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada applicable therein.

12. Entire Agreement
This Agreement (together with the Purchase Agreement and any confidentiality/IP agreements) constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties.

FOUNDER EXIT & RELEASE AGREEMENT
This Founder Exit & Release Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made as of [Date] by and between:

[Company Name], an Ontario corporation with its principal place of business at [Address] (the “Company”), and

[Founder Name], of [Address] (the “Founder”).

1. Resignation
The Founder hereby resigns from all positions held with the Company, including Chief Financial Officer, director, and officer roles, effective as of [Date] (the “Effective Date”).

2. Equity Buyback
The Parties acknowledge that the Founder’s shares in the Company have been repurchased pursuant to the Share Purchase Agreement dated [Date] (the “Purchase Agreement”), and that no further payments or obligations exist under or in connection with such shares.

3. Mutual Release
Subject to the exceptions set forth herein, each Party, on behalf of itself and its affiliates, heirs, executors, administrators, successors, and assigns, hereby fully releases and forever discharges the other Party from any and all claims, demands, causes of action, liabilities, or obligations of any kind, whether known or unknown, arising prior to the Effective Date, including but not limited to claims relating to employment, equity, fiduciary duties, or corporate governance.

4. Existing Obligations
The Founder acknowledges and agrees that all obligations under any previously executed Non‑Disclosure, Confidentiality, or Intellectual Property Assignment Agreements with the Company remain in full force and effect.

5. Return of Company Property
The Founder agrees to return all Company property, including but not limited to documents, electronic files, devices, keys, passwords, and any other materials belonging to the Company, no later than [Date].

6. Non‑Solicitation
For a period of 12 months following the Effective Date, the Founder shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, recruit, or induce any employee, contractor, or client of the Company to terminate or modify their relationship with the Company.

7. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada applicable therein.

8. Entire Agreement
This Agreement, together with the Purchase Agreement and any previously executed NDA or IP assignment agreements, constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes any prior agreements or understandings, written or oral, relating thereto.

9. Counterparts
This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument. Signatures delivered by electronic means shall be deemed originals.
