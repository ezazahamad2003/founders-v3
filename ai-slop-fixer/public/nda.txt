Absolutely! Since you’re an early-stage startup in Ontario, the NDA should comply with Canadian law (specifically Ontario’s contractual law) and reflect typical market standards for technology service providers. Here’s a comprehensive draft you can adapt:

NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (NDA)

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into as of [Date] (“Effective Date”) by and between:

Disclosing Party: [Your Company Name], an Ontario corporation with its principal place of business at [Address] (“Disclosing Party”); and

Receiving Party: [Service Provider Name], a [jurisdiction] corporation with its principal place of business at [Address] (“Receiving Party”).

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Disclosing Party possesses certain confidential and proprietary information related to its technology, business plans, and operations;

WHEREAS, the Receiving Party may receive such information in connection with discussions regarding potential business collaboration or service provision;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definition of Confidential Information
For purposes of this Agreement, “Confidential Information” means all information disclosed by the Disclosing Party, whether in written, oral, electronic, or other form, including but not limited to:

business plans, strategies, financial information;

technology, software, source code, algorithms, processes, and designs;

client lists, supplier information, and other proprietary business information;

any information marked as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

Confidential Information does not include information that:

is or becomes publicly available without breach of this Agreement;

is lawfully obtained from a third party without restriction;

is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information.

2. Obligations of Receiving Party
The Receiving Party shall:
a) Maintain all Confidential Information in strict confidence;
b) Use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of evaluating or performing services for the Disclosing Party;
c) Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party;
d) Take all reasonable precautions to prevent unauthorized access to the Confidential Information.

3. Term
This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of [2 years] from the date of disclosure of the Confidential Information, unless terminated earlier by mutual written consent.

The confidentiality obligations shall survive the termination of this Agreement for [3 years].

4. Return or Destruction of Materials
Upon request of the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all materials containing Confidential Information, including copies, notes, or summaries.

5. No License or Ownership
Nothing in this Agreement grants any license, rights, or ownership in any intellectual property of the Disclosing Party. All Confidential Information remains the exclusive property of the Disclosing Party.

6. Remedies
The Receiving Party acknowledges that unauthorized disclosure may cause irreparable harm to the Disclosing Party. In addition to any other remedies available at law or equity, the Disclosing Party may seek injunctive relief to prevent such disclosure.

7. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada applicable therein. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Ontario for any disputes arising under this Agreement.

8. Miscellaneous
a) Entire Agreement: This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the subject matter.
b) Amendments: Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both parties.
c) Severability: If any provision is found invalid, the remainder shall remain in effect.
d) Waiver: Failure to enforce any provision does not constitute a waiver of future enforcement.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Non-Disclosure Agreement as of the Effective Date.

Perfect! Since your main priority is strong IP protection, we can tighten the NDA specifically around ownership and rights to anything the service provider creates, while keeping it concise. Here’s a startup-friendly, mutual NDA with strong IP clauses:

MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AGREEMENT (NDA)

Effective Date: [Date]

Parties:

Party A: [Your Company Name], Ontario corporation, [Address]

Party B: [Service Provider Name], [jurisdiction] corporation, [Address]

1. Confidential Information
“Confidential Information” means any non-public information disclosed by either party relating to business, technology, software, processes, clients, finances, or other proprietary information, whether written, electronic, or oral.

Exclusions: Information is not confidential if it:

Is publicly available without breach;

Was lawfully obtained from a third party;

Is independently developed without using the other party’s Confidential Information.

2. Obligations
Each party agrees to:

Keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential;

Use it only for evaluating or performing services under discussion;

Not disclose it without prior written consent;

Take reasonable steps to prevent unauthorized disclosure.

3. Intellectual Property
3.1 Pre-Existing IP: Each party retains all rights, title, and interest in intellectual property it owned or developed before this NDA.

3.2 Deliverables / Work-for-Hire:

All work, inventions, designs, software, documentation, or other deliverables created by a party for the other party under any services arrangement (the “Deliverables”) are the exclusive property of the receiving party (i.e., your startup).

The creating party assigns all rights, title, and interest in the Deliverables to the receiving party, including all intellectual property rights worldwide.

The creating party agrees to execute any documents reasonably necessary to confirm, perfect, or enforce this ownership.

3.3 Moral Rights: The creating party waives any moral rights in the Deliverables to the extent permitted by law.

3.4 Residual Knowledge: The creating party may retain general knowledge, skills, and experience gained, but may not use or disclose any Confidential Information or Deliverables in future projects.

3.5 No License: Except as expressly stated, no license or rights to the other party’s IP are granted.

4. Return of Materials
Upon request, each party shall return or destroy all materials containing the other party’s Confidential Information.

5. Term
This NDA applies from the Effective Date for 2 years. Confidentiality and IP obligations survive indefinitely for IP and 3 years for other Confidential Information.

6. Governing Law
This NDA is governed by the laws of Ontario, Canada, and the parties submit to the courts of Ontario for any disputes.
